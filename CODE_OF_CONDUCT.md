# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Aegis Mesh a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, constructive feedback, respect for differing viewpoints, responsibility for mistakes, and focus on what is best for the community. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, sexualized attention, insults, threats, publishing private information, and other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and interactions that violate this Code and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for harmful behavior. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainer contact mechanisms. Maintainers will respect the reporter's privacy and apply proportionate consequences.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html
