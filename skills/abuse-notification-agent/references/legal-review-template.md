# Jurisdiction-neutral legal review template

This file is a deployment checklist, not legal advice and not a source of law. Aegis Mesh ships with no active jurisdiction, recipient, channel, citation, or authorization to send.

Before enabling an external notification, a qualified reviewer must record:

- the applicable jurisdictions and why they apply;
- the current primary legal or contractual sources;
- the lawful purpose and minimum necessary disclosure;
- retention, privacy, cross-border transfer, legal-hold, and authority-coordination constraints;
- the verified recipient and appeal or correction channel;
- the review date, expiry date, reviewer identity, and approved template version.

Use conditional factual language. Do not allege criminality or identify a natural person. The final reviewed profile must be deployment-owned, versioned, time-bounded, and bound to the frozen notification body. If any field is absent or stale, notification remains disabled.
